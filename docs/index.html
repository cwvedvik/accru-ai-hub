Accru AI Hub

Design system & component style guide

The buildable design language for the Accru AI Hub — a governed catalogue of AI apps, agents, skills, prompts and training for the group's firms across Norway, Sweden, Denmark and the UK. Editorial, warm, and considered: an app store made by people who understand accounting, not an internal SharePoint with a new logo.

Serif on cream

Gelasio headings on a cream canvas (never white) is the whole brand argument — editorial and trustworthy.

Orange acts, red structures

Orange owns action; deep red owns structure. Neither is decoration.

WCAG 2.2 AA

A workplace tool in four countries. Accessibility is a legal requirement, treated as craft.

Foundation

Color

Bright --brand-orange fails AA as text or a small control (~3.4:1), so functional orange is the darker --action #b8501c. Bright orange is reserved for display text ≥24px, decoration, and chart fills.

Elevation

A small, disciplined ladder. Cards are grounded; they lift, they never float. Every card keeps its 1px rule regardless of shadow.

Foundation

Motion

Restrained and named. --dur-fast 120ms / --dur-base 180ms / --dur-slow 240ms, --ease-out cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1). Nothing loops; nothing moves without a user action. Honours prefers-reduced-motion.

This drawer opens over --dur-base with a 4–8px rise and fade — the same motion the scope panel and dropdowns use.
Foundation

Iconography

Phosphor Icons (regular), 20px default. Country flags via flag-icons. No emoji as UI. Icons paired with labels for anything actionable.

Non-negotiable

Accessibility & trust

WCAG 2.2 AA is a legal requirement in all four countries and is treated as craft, not compliance.
  • Functional orange is --action #b8501c; bright #dc6834 only ≥24px or decorative.
  • Two-tone :focus-visible ring — tab through this page to see it. Passes the 3:1 non-text rule.
  • Colour is never the only signal — health, ratings and charts all carry a label or icon.
  • Hit targets ≥44×44px; command palette and submission wizard fully keyboard-operable.
  • One date format everywhere: 12 Jun 2026 (no ambiguous 06/12 across countries).
  • Motion honours prefers-reduced-motion.

This style guide is a living reference for the v0.3 spec. Full detail lives in the UX & interface spec and technical architecture.
